Oligarch Travel — Website Design

Vision: a booking page as clear as the big marketplaces — a visitor lands, searches, and books in under a minute. One dominant search bar over the Hagia Sophia hero, tour cards that show photo, rating and price at a glance, and proof (real TripAdvisor / Viator / Airbnb numbers) printed on the cards themselves. Clarity first; everything else is quiet.

Why this palette: clean white and dark navy read as trustworthy and scannable on a phone in bright sunlight. There is one orange family with two shades by role: the bright #FF5533 is a graphic accent (stars, large non-text marks), while every surface that carries text — the search button, booking buttons, badge chips — uses the deep shade #C93A1B, so every label passes WCAG AA (5.12:1) and the eye still always knows the next step.

Usage rules

  • Orange means action. It appears only on the search button, booking and cart buttons, the Badge-of-Excellence chip, rating stars and interactive hover/focus states — never as decoration (no orange borders, dividers or section backgrounds).
  • Bright orange never carries text. #FF5533 is 3.18:1 on white, so it is graphics-only; any orange surface with text, and any orange text, uses Deep #C93A1B (5.12:1, AA). Hover deepens to #A93213 (6.66:1).
  • Cards scan in one fixed order: photo → title → ★ rating → duration / meta → price. Same order on every card, in every language.
  • One geometry: 12px radius on cards and panels, 9px on buttons, one resting shadow (0 2px 10px, navy 8%) and one lifted shadow (0 14px 34px, navy 18%) for hover and the hero search bar.
  • Contrast floor: every text/background pair is ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) — Ink on White 14.13:1, Ink Soft on White 5.40:1, Ink Soft on Mist 5.03:1, White on Deep Orange 5.12:1.
